Privacy Policy

Span: Countdown & Time Tracker

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Overview

Span has no accounts. Your spans and trackers are stored on your device, and we never see them. Span counts how its features are used, in a way that isn't tied to you, and you can turn that off in Settings. A few features reach outside services when you use them. This page lists all of them. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Data Storage

Your spans, trackers, notes, tags and preferences are stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. They don't leave your device unless you back them up, share a span, or use a feature described below.

iCloud Backup

Span offers an optional manual iCloud backup. When you use it, your span data is saved to your personal iCloud Drive account. Apple manages this data under Apple's Privacy Policy, and we don't have access to it.

Usage Data

Unless you turn it off, Span sends a small set of counts to PostHog, our analytics provider, so we can see which features are used and improve them:

  • When the app opens and closes, and when it's installed or updated
  • When you add a span or tracker, or log a tracker, and whether a span repeats and how it was added
  • When you open certain features, such as Timeline or On This Day
  • Which Span Pro screens you reach, whether a purchase or restore went through, and whether you have Span Pro
  • Your app version, iOS version, device model, screen size, language, time zone, and whether you are on Wi-Fi or cellular
  • A random ID created by the app, used to count repeat visits. It isn't connected to your name, Apple Account or anything else about you

These counts never include your span or tracker names, emoji, notes, tags, dates, ZIP codes, tracker logs, or anything else you type. Span doesn't record your screen. We've configured PostHog to discard your IP address and not to work out your location from it.

To turn this off, go to Settings in Span and switch off Share usage data. Span stops sending counts right away, and turning it back on starts with a new random ID.

PostHog stores these counts in the United States for up to one year. See PostHog's Privacy Policy. Because the counts aren't tied to you, we can't find or delete one person's counts. If you have a question about this, contact us below.

Other Services Span Uses

  • Apple. StoreKit handles purchases, and MapKit turns a ZIP code or an imported event's location into coordinates. Both are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Open-Meteo. If you add a ZIP code to a span, Span sends its coordinates and date to Open-Meteo to show the weather. It doesn't send the span's name. Open-Meteo can see your IP address, as any website can.
  • Wikimedia. On This Day, in the app and its widget, asks Wikimedia for events on a month and day. Wikimedia can see your IP address too.

Shared Links

When you share a span as a link, the span's name and date, plus any emoji, tags and notes, are written into the link itself. Anyone with the link can read them. Links open through a page we host on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process the link when someone opens it. We don't keep a copy.

Calendar

If you import from Calendar, Span asks for access to your events. It reads them on your device and keeps only the events you choose.

Notifications

Span can send local notifications about your spans and trackers. They're scheduled on your device and don't go through any server of ours.

What We Don't Collect

  • No names, email addresses or accounts
  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other apps or websites
  • No cookies or web tracking in the app
  • No GPS or device location
  • No contacts or photos

Children's Privacy

Span isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.
